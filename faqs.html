What are the concentrations and focus areas in Applied Physics?
Applied Physics master's students have the option to declare a concentration as part of their degree. Concentrations are listed on your official transcript.

• Materials and Condensed Matter: Combine concepts from the Applied Physics, Electrical and Computer Engineering, and Materials Science and Engineering programs for a well-rounded, holistic approach to applied physics.

• Applied Optics and Photonics: Study applied physics, electrical and computer engineering and photonics concepts for a defined approach for the practical application of light. Listed as Photonics in the academic catalog.

The Applied Physics program also offers the following focus areas. Focus areas are unofficial and are not listed on your transcripts.

• Space and Earth Science
• Quantum Engineering and Technology (forthcoming)

Full descriptions are on the Concentrations and Focus Areas page. Course requirements are on the Courses page.
What important dates do I need to know?
The official Academic Calendar is here: ep.jhu.edu/student-services/academic-calendar. It shows registration open dates, first day of classes, tuition payment deadlines, drop/refund schedules, and graduation application deadlines.
How do I get help with advising or student services?
Start at the Student Services hub. For academic success support email ep-studentsuccess@jhu.edu. Your faculty advisor is available to help with course planning and degree progress. Degree audits are completed electronically in Stellic.
Who do I contact for program-specific questions?
For general student success questions, email ep-studentsuccess@jhu.edu. Current student FAQs are also available at ep.jhu.edu/current-students/faqs.
